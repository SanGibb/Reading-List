- Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09449

## TL;DR
Sekai2 is a long-horizon, trajectory-annotated real-world video corpus designed for camera-controllable and persistent interactive world modeling, including loop-and-revisit panoramic sequences that directly supervise spatial memory.

## Novelty

The corpus combines long duration, camera trajectories, temporal semantics, and loop/revisit supervision at unusual scale.

## Contributions
- 128,892 clips and 2,826 hours from 10,428 source videos.
- Camera trajectories plus hierarchical, temporally grounded motion/scene annotations.
- 982 non-linear panoramic sequences with loops and revisits.

## Task
Pretraining and evaluating long-horizon video generation, controllable camera motion, spatial memory, and persistent world representations.

## Data
Coverage spans 113 countries or regions and 649,597 temporally grounded annotation segments; 43,594 segments reach two minutes under the paper's decomposition.

## Method
A multi-source curation pipeline estimates/releases camera trajectories, decomposes long footage, and annotates subject motion, environmental dynamics, static content, and camera behavior.

## Evidence
Corpus-scale analyses report broad geographic/semantic coverage, varied trajectories, and low temporal caption redundancy. The loop/revisit subset is especially relevant to persistent world-state evaluation.

## Limitations
- Web-derived footage inherits geographic, capture-style, and licensing biases.
- Camera poses may be noisy and are not equivalent to embodiment/action labels.
- Dataset access terms and complete release artifacts need verification.

## Project Relevance
Provides long-horizon camera/action metadata and revisit supervision missing from short robot clips; useful for spatial-memory and drift evaluation.

## Reproduction / Follow-up
Audit licensing and pose accuracy, sample long clips for annotation quality, and define a loop-closure benchmark before using the corpus.
