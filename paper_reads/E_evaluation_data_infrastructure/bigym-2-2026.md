## TL;DR

Places coding-agent policies and learned humanoid controllers in the same open, replayable evaluation harness. Strong infrastructure for WholeBodyController, VRDemonstration, HiddenSeed, AgentWrittenPolicy, ReplayTrace, and humanoid task evaluation. Central results are author-reported and were not independently reproduced in this collection run.

## Contributions

1. A common whole-body controller and observation/action interface compare VLA fine-tuning, imitation, demo-driven RL, and code-writing agents under fixed seeds.
2. 20 Unitree G1 household tasks, 60 VR demonstrations per task, 1,200 demonstrations, and 5,400 coding-agent evaluation episodes.
3. The open release includes code, 20 tasks, 1,200 demonstrations, 5,400 agent episodes, and browser-based 3D replay.
