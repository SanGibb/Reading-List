## TL;DR

AD-WM trains latent dynamics to preserve differences among counterfactual actions—the information MPC needs but ordinary factual prediction can discard. Large hard-start and Franka gains make the argument compelling, although real-robot evidence remains narrow.

## Novelty

- Separates factual next-state accuracy from counterfactual action discrimination.
- Adds inverse-dynamics and normalized action-recovery objectives, discarded at inference.
- Proposes CEM-aligned elite regret as a more planning-relevant diagnostic.

## Contributions

1. Action-discriminative joint-embedding dynamics.
2. Matched closed-loop studies showing prediction error can mis-rank planners.
3. Zero-shot Franka transfer with frozen V-JEPA 2 features and DROID post-training.

## Task

- Input: current visual latent and candidate action sequence.
- Output: predicted latent transitions for MPC scoring.
- Setting: OGBench-Cube, five simulated tasks, and Franka pick-and-place.
- Success criterion: closed-loop task success under counterfactual search.

## Data

- OGBench and matched simulation datasets; DROID is used for post-training before Franka transfer.

## Method

- Residual latent dynamics predict transitions.
- Predictor-level inverse and normalized recovery heads retain action information.
- CEM searches candidate sequences; auxiliary heads vanish at deployment.

## Evidence

- Mean success improves over the reproduced baseline in four of five simulation environments.
- Elite regret tracks closed-loop ordering better than factual error or whole-bank ranking.

## Limitations

- Real-world evaluation centers on basic pick-and-place.
- Action recovery requires well-specified action labels and may inherit dataset bias.

## Project Relevance

AD-WM motivates `CounterfactualAction`, `ActionSeparability`, `EliteRegret`, and planning-aligned validation fields for learned world representations.

## Reproduction / Follow-up

- Reproduce matched baselines before citing the large percentage-point gains.
- Test whether the objectives scale to contact-rich and long-horizon action spaces.
