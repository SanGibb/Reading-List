## TL;DR

XR-1: Towards Versatile Vision-Language-Action Models via Learning Unified Vision-Motion Representations is included because it addresses **versatile robot manipulation across diverse embodiments and environments** with a method centered on **Unified Vision-Motion Codes learned by dual-branch VQ-VAE with three-stage VLA training**. For our repository, the important point is not venue alone but that the work gives concrete evidence for vision-language-action models, robot foundation policies, and action-conditioned world models. Main caveat: Large-scale rollout claims are official; independent reproduction and checkpoint availability should be checked.

## Novelty

- What is actually new: aligns visual dynamics and robot motion into a shared intermediate code for scalable VLA learning.
