## TL;DR

Octo is the open robot-policy baseline that many newer VLA papers compare against. It is critical for our knowledge base because it provides a practical policy interface for testing whether generated tasks/scenes transfer to real manipulation policies.

## Novelty

- What is actually new: open generalist policy recipe with flexible observation/action spaces and broad finetuning support.
- Difference from prior work: it adds executable, interactive, physical, spatial, or policy-facing structure rather than stopping at static visual quality.
- Why the delta matters: our repository needs works that can become fields, validators, baselines, or generation targets for interactive embodied worlds.

## Contributions

1. Releases Octo-Small and Octo-Base open models.
2. Trains on a broad Open X-Embodiment mixture.
3. Evaluates on 9 real robot setups across 4 institutions.
