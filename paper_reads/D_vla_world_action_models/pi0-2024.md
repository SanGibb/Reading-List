## TL;DR

pi0 is one of the most visually convincing VLA systems for real robot dexterity. It is not fully open in the same way as OpenVLA/Octo, but its demos and flow-action formulation are important for setting the quality bar.

## Novelty

- What is actually new: uses continuous flow-matching action generation to scale VLM-backed robot policies to dexterous control.
- Difference from prior work: it adds executable, interactive, physical, spatial, or policy-facing structure rather than stopping at static visual quality.
- Why the delta matters: our repository needs works that can become fields, validators, baselines, or generation targets for interactive embodied worlds.

## Contributions

1. Introduces a VLA flow architecture for high-frequency continuous actions.
2. Trains across 8 robot platforms and diverse dexterous tasks.
3. Shows strong real-world demos including laundry folding, table bussing, and box assembly.
