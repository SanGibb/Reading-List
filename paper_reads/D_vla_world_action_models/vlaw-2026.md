# VLAW: Iterative Co-Improvement of Vision-Language-Action Policy and World Model

## Source Links

- Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12063
- OpenReview: https://openreview.net/pdf?id=Ro0eQ0ly3q
- Author research page: https://lucys0.github.io/

## TL;DR

VLAW closes a practical learning loop between a VLA and an action-conditioned video world model. Real policy successes and failures repair the simulator's weak contact physics; the repaired simulator generates supplemental rollouts that improve the policy. The paper reports a 39.2-point gain over the base policy, including 11.6 points from synthetic rollouts.

## Novelty

Rather than freezing the learned simulator, VLAW iteratively exposes it to the same failure distribution that matters to the deployed policy, then returns its improved rollouts to policy training.

## Contributions

1. An alternating policy/world-model improvement loop based on small real rollout batches.
2. Failure-rich world-model adaptation with DROID co-training for regularization.
3. Synthetic policy-training rollouts from the updated simulator.
4. Real-robot evidence isolating the gain attributable to generated rollouts.

## Task Definition

Improve language-conditioned real-robot manipulation when real online interaction is scarce and the initial action-conditioned world model is unreliable on contact-rich failures.

## Data / Dataset / Benchmark

The loop uses real policy rollouts, original DROID data, and world-model-generated trajectories across multiple downstream real-robot manipulation tasks.

## Method / Pipeline

Deploy the current policy, label rollout outcomes with a vision-language reward model, adapt Ctrl-World using collected successes and failures while retaining DROID regularization, generate synthetic rollouts, and update the VLA. Repeat the cycle.

## Evidence / Metrics / Qualitative Results

Source-reported comparisons show that online failure data improves small-contact fidelity and that generated rollouts add policy benefit beyond real-only updates. Paper figures provide side-by-side trajectories and video-quality comparisons sufficient for an adequate visual judgment.

## Limitations

The public project surface remains sparse, the loop still requires real deployments, reward-model errors can contaminate labels, and gains are shown on a limited task/robot set. Generated data is useful only after targeted world-model repair.

## Project Relevance

VLAW is a direct reference architecture for maintaining a coupled executable policy and learned simulator through evidence gathered at their shared failure boundary.

## Reproduction / Follow-up Notes
