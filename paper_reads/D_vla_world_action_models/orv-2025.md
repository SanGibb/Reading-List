## TL;DR

ORV conditions robot-video generation on chunked 7-DoF actions and dense renderings of 4D semantic occupancy. This reduces the gap between sparse controls and pixels, enabling controllable single/multi-view videos and sim-to-real appearance transfer. The project provides broad galleries; the cost is occupancy construction at T by 400 by 400 by 400 resolution.

## Novelty

- Combines action modulation with occupancy-derived visual priors.
- Moves beyond coarse actions and single-view RGB generation.
- Targets control alignment, geometry, and multiview consistency together.

## Contributions

1. Introduces Action-Expert AdaLN plus occupancy soft guidance.
2. Curates ORV-Data for semantic occupancy sequences.
3. Demonstrates generation across BridgeV2, DROID, RT-1 and sim-to-real augmentation.

- Output: controllable robot video across one or more views.

- Ablations: action and occupancy components.

- Reproducibility signals: paper, public code, large qualitative surface.

## Limitations

- Occupancy preprocessing/storage is expensive.
- Downstream gains are modest and task-limited.
- Web videos are 320x480; dataset scale and metrics were not reproduced.

## Project Relevance

- Dense physical-state guidance for controllable robot-video worlds.
- Reusable fields: ActionChunk, SemanticOccupancy4D, ViewSet, ControlAlignment.
- Baseline role: occupancy-conditioned robot video data engine.
- Compare sparse controls against dense executable-state conditions.
