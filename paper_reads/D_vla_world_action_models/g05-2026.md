# G0.5: One Autoregressive Stream for Robot Reasoning and Action

## Source Links
- Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11739
- Official project and demos: https://opengalaxea.github.io/G05/
- Code/checkpoints: https://github.com/OpenGalaxea/GalaxeaVLA

## TL;DR
G0.5 replaces the usual VLM-plus-action-expert split with one autoregressive decoder that emits grounded reasoning and cross-embodiment action tokens. Its breadth of simulation and real-robot evaluation, plus inspectable failure cases and rollouts, makes it a strong generalist VLA reference.

## Novelty

Reasoning and cross-embodiment action generation share one autoregressive vocabulary, decoder, and training objective.

## Contributions
- A learned cross-embodiment action codec maps heterogeneous robot controls into one token vocabulary.
- Native chain-of-thought interleaves task decomposition, object grounding, action hints, and actions.
- Visual memory injects multi-second history without a separate planner.

## Task
Language-conditioned closed-loop manipulation across fixed-base and mobile bimanual robots, including long-horizon household tasks.

## Data
Large mixed robot datasets and VQA data; evaluations cover R1 Lite/Pro, BEHAVIOR-1K, DROID transfer, LIBERO, RoboTwin 2.0, and SimplerEnv-Bridge.

## Method
Multi-view RGB, embodiment id, instruction, and proprioception are serialized into a single transformer stream. A residual-vector-quantized ActionCodec encodes per-part controls; one next-token loss supervises reasoning and actions.

## Evidence
- BEHAVIOR-1K task score 31.4%, versus 26.3% for the reported pi0.5 baseline.
- Zero-shot DROID transfer reports 82.5%; SimplerEnv-Bridge reports 87.3%.
- Qualitative galleries include successful and failed real-robot episodes across two embodiments.

## Limitations
- Results are author-reported and the training mixture is only coarsely described.
- Autoregressive action decoding can be slower than continuous experts; an optional flow head changes the pure single-stream story.
- Prompt-driven emergent behaviors are demonstrated on small samples.

## Project Relevance
Useful baseline for unified reasoning/action tokens, cross-embodiment control schemas, long-horizon memory, and readable policy traces.

## Reproduction / Follow-up
