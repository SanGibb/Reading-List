## TL;DR

Infers unsupervised environment factors such as friction, mass, and rules through test-time latent optimization. Direct reference for EnvironmentLatent, HiddenPhysicalProperty, TestInteraction, OnlineAdaptation, and action-conditioned dynamics. Main caveat: the release is new and its reported results have not been independently reproduced.

## Novelty

- What is actually new: Infers unsupervised environment factors such as friction, mass, and rules through test-time latent optimization.
- Difference from prior work: Alternates world-model and environment-latent optimization during training, then adapts a new compact latent from one or more test interactions while freezing the model.
- Why the delta matters: Direct reference for EnvironmentLatent, HiddenPhysicalProperty, TestInteraction, OnlineAdaptation, and action-conditioned dynamics.

## Contributions

1. Alternates world-model and environment-latent optimization during training, then adapts a new compact latent from one or more test interactions while freezing the model.
2. Seven simulated settings and four real robotic tasks varying latent physical properties and environment rules.
3. Primary paper reports object-prediction error falling from 15.20 to 7.00 and action success rising from 33.73% to 72.96%.
