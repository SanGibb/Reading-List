## TL;DR

GEM-4D distills dense 4D correspondence from a frozen geometry foundation model into a video DiT, discards the geometry branch at inference, and converts generated futures into robot commands with adaptive inverse dynamics. It reports real manipulation success rising from 61% to 81%; absent public code is the main reproducibility gap.

## Novelty

- Geometry-enhanced velocity alignment inside a single-stream video world model.
- Distills correspondence during training with no extra inference branch.
- Improves geometric action utility without added rollout cost.

## Contributions

1. Injects foundation-model correspondence supervision into video generation.
2. Adds grounding, tracking correction, and inverse kinematics.
3. Evaluates across four datasets and real UF ARM manipulation.
